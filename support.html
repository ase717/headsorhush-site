Heads or Hush: Support

Need help with Heads or Hush? Write to asestudio21@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

When you write, please tell us your phone model, your iOS version, the app version (Settings in your phone, then Heads or Hush) and what happened.

Common questions

How do I play?
Choose a category, add 3 to 12 players and pick a tone and the number of rounds. Pass the phone around: when it is your turn, hold the card to read your secret question, let go, and say out loud the name of the one person it makes you think of. Do not say the question. Then flip the coin: on heads the question is revealed to everyone, on tails it stays a secret forever. You can see the steps again anytime from "How to play" on the home screen.

Why can I not see the question again?
That is the game. The card shows the question only while you hold it, and a question on a tails result is never shown again.

Is the coin really fair?
Yes. Every flip is a fair 50/50, decided at the moment you press the button. It does not depend on the question, the player or earlier flips.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings inside the app and tap "Restore purchases", or tap "Restore purchases" on the subscription screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription or the free trial?
Open Settings inside the app and tap "Manage subscription", or go to your App Store subscriptions yourself. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I change the language?
Yes, in Settings inside the app: English or Turkish.

The game does not start or says the category is locked.
Starting a game needs an internet connection. A premium category needs an active subscription; tap "Restore purchases" if you already subscribed. Once a game has started it keeps working offline.

What data does the app collect?
Very little, and never player names or questions. See the Privacy Policy.
